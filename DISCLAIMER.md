# Disclaimer

This repository is an educational and professional portfolio case study based on a fictional organisation and fictional recruitment AI scenario.

It demonstrates the author's learning and practical application of AI governance concepts using the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0). It is not evidence of an actual enterprise implementation, independent audit, vendor certification, legal opinion, regulatory approval or formal compliance assessment.

All ratings, findings, controls and governance decisions are illustrative. They must be validated against real systems, data, evidence, organisational risk appetite and applicable law before use in practice.

NIST AI RMF is a risk-management framework and does not prescribe a universal numerical risk scale or universal high/medium/low risk classification. Legal obligations vary by jurisdiction and may change over time. Obtain qualified legal, privacy, security and compliance advice for real-world decisions.
