# AI Risk Register – Recruitment AI

**Scenario:** GlobalTech Services Ltd. (fictional)  
**Use case:** Third-party recruitment screening and ranking  
**Status:** Illustrative initial register; owners and ratings require validation

| ID | Risk statement | Initial rating | Suggested owner | Treatment / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-001 | Career breaks may lower candidate rankings without job-related justification | High | HR Business Owner | Root-cause analysis, fairness tests, remediation and retest |
| AI-002 | Educational institution or other proxy features may disadvantage candidates | High | HR / AI System Owner | Feature relevance review and outcome testing |
| AI-003 | Vendor may use candidate data for unauthorised secondary purposes | High | Privacy / Procurement | Processing review, contractual restrictions and audit evidence |
| AI-004 | Candidate data may be retained longer than justified | High | HR / Privacy | Retention schedule, deletion controls and evidence |
| AI-005 | Recruiters may not understand or challenge AI rankings | High | HR Business Owner | Explanations, training, review workflow and override monitoring |
| AI-006 | Candidate data may be accessed or disclosed without authorisation | High | Information Security | Access control, encryption, testing and incident controls |
| AI-007 | Recruiters may over-rely on rankings or review only top-ranked candidates | High | HR Business Owner | Meaningful review, workflow testing and audit trail |
| AI-008 | Vendor model changes may alter outcomes without reassessment | High | AI System Owner | Change notification, impact assessment and approval |

## Register fields to maintain

For each risk, maintain:
- Risk description and affected stakeholders.
- Inherent likelihood and impact.
- Existing controls and control owner.
- Treatment actions, due dates and status.
- Residual likelihood and impact.
- Evidence and assessment date.
- Risk acceptance decision and authorised approver.
- Monitoring indicators and escalation triggers.

**Rating note:** “High” is an illustrative internal rating. The organisation must define its own rating methodology. NIST AI RMF does not prescribe this register format or universal risk tiers. Inherent and residual ratings must not be conflated.
